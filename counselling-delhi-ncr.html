Support for Delhi NCR

Delhi NCR concentrates a particular kind of pressure. High-performance workplaces, long hours, heavy traffic, and a large population who moved here for work and left their support network behind. People often arrive at counselling here saying some version of the same thing: everything looks fine from outside, and it does not feel fine.

What People Across NCR Bring to Sessions

💼

Workplace Pressure and Burnout

Always-on cultures, unclear expectations, and performance anxiety that follows you home.

🌑

Isolation After Relocating

Building a life in a new city, and the loneliness that persists even with a full calendar.

👥

Social and Performance Anxiety

Presentations, meetings, networking, and the replay afterwards.

💬

Relationship and Family Strain

Conflict that repeats, distance from family, and conversations that keep becoming arguments.

One Region, No Commute

NCR traffic makes a fixed weekly appointment across the city genuinely difficult to sustain. Consistency matters more than almost anything else in counselling, and travel is what most often breaks it. All sessions run online over secure video, so where you are in the region does not matter — Delhi, Gurgaon, Faridabad, Noida or Ghaziabad. PsySupport is an online counselling service and does not operate a walk-in office anywhere in NCR.
