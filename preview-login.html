Why not cash

Prize money over ₹10,000 means deducting 30% TDS under Section 194B, collecting PAN details and filing returns. Prize competitions also run into state-level rules. Beyond the paperwork, cash attracts people who want cash — they enter, they win, they never book. A free session attracts people who want a session.

The rules this has to follow

Two separate things are happening here, and keeping them apart is what makes the whole design workable. The competition runs on your server because a quiz score is ordinary data. The diary stays on the phone because a mood record is not. They never meet.

  • No box is ever pre-ticked, and each permission is asked separately
  • Only call people who ticked may contact — having the number is not permission
  • Only show names of people who ticked show my name
  • Leads are deleted after their session, as you decided
  • Anyone can ask to be removed, and you have to do it
  • The competition needs a published terms page — who can enter, how winners are picked, how prizes are given
