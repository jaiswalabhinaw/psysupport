About PsySupport
Who We Are & How We Help

Our Story

PsySupport was created to make structured emotional support accessible and approachable — without labels, judgement, or the stigma that often surrounds mental health conversations. We work with individuals in Noida and across India who are dealing with anxiety, overthinking, low mood, and everyday emotional challenges.

About Your Counsellor

PsySupport is led by Abhinaw, a graduate in psychology and a mental health counsellor providing counselling in Noida and online across India.

Sessions focus on anxiety, overthinking, low mood, work-related stress and the everyday emotional challenges people carry without naming them. The approach is structured and non-judgemental, and deliberately avoids labelling or treating anyone as a patient to be diagnosed.

If you would like to ask about qualifications, approach or whether counselling is the right fit for what you are facing, you are welcome to ask before booking — message us on WhatsApp or call +91 87566 80477.
