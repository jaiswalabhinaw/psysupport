Understand Your Mental Health

Free, validated assessment tools to help you gain insight into your emotional wellbeing. These screening tests are used by mental health professionals worldwide.

🧠 Depression Screening

⏱️ 2 min
❓ 9 questions

The PHQ-9 is a validated tool used worldwide to screen for depression. Understand if you may be experiencing depressive symptoms.

😰 Anxiety Screening

⏱️ 2 min
❓ 7 questions

The GAD-7 screens for generalized anxiety disorder. Check if anxiety might be affecting your daily life and wellbeing.

💪 Stress Assessment

⏱️ 1 min
❓ 4 questions

Quick assessment of your current stress levels and how you're coping with everyday pressures in life.

PHQ-9: Depression Screening

Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by the following problems?

1. Little interest or pleasure in doing things
2. Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless
3. Trouble falling or staying asleep, or sleeping too much
4. Feeling tired or having little energy
5. Poor appetite or overeating
6. Feeling bad about yourself — or that you're a failure or have let yourself or your family down
7. Trouble concentrating on things, such as reading the newspaper or watching television
8. Moving or speaking so slowly that others have noticed? Or the opposite — being so fidgety or restless that you've been moving around more than usual
9. Thoughts that you would be better off dead, or of hurting yourself

GAD-7: Anxiety Screening

Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by the following problems?

1. Feeling nervous, anxious or on edge
2. Not being able to stop or control worrying
3. Worrying too much about different things
4. Trouble relaxing
5. Being so restless that it's hard to sit still
6. Becoming easily annoyed or irritable
7. Feeling afraid as if something awful might happen

Stress Assessment

How much do the following statements apply to you right now?

1. I feel overwhelmed by my responsibilities
2. I have difficulty managing my stress effectively
3. Stress is negatively affecting my work, relationships or health
4. I feel I need support to better manage my stress
