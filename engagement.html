Why bother

What a diary gives you that memory does not

A bad week is not a bad month

When things are hard, the hard days are the ones you remember. Written down, most people find the picture is less uniform than it felt — and the good days become evidence rather than a fluke.

Causes stop being invisible

Sleep, a particular weekday, one recurring situation. These are almost impossible to spot from inside the week, and obvious across eight of them.

It makes the first session easier

Counsellors ask what has been happening. Arriving with two months of actual days beats trying to reconstruct it under pressure, and it saves a session's worth of guessing.

Keeping your diary

Two minutes now saves two months later

A browser can clear its own storage — phones do it to free space, and iPhones do it automatically if you go a few weeks without opening a site. Either of these stops that happening.

Add the diary to your home screen

The surest fix, and it opens like an app

Installed to your home screen, the diary gets storage the browser will not quietly clear, it opens in one tap without the address bar, and it works with no signal. Nothing is uploaded — it is the same page, kept properly.

On iPhone or iPad
  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (the square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. The diary appears as an icon with your other apps.

It has to be Safari — Chrome on iPhone cannot install pages.

On Android
  1. Open the browser menu (the three dots, top right).
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Confirm. The diary appears with your other apps.

✓ You are running the installed diary. Your entries are kept properly here.

Or keep a backup copy

Also how you move the diary to a new phone

A backup is a small file holding everything you have logged. Save it somewhere you keep things — it restores onto any phone or computer, including a new one.

Restoring merges the backup with anything already here. Where both have the same day, the backup wins.

Your data

The diary lives in this browser only. It is not backed up, and clearing your browsing data, switching phone or opening this page in a private window will lose it. There is no account and no copy on our side.

When you want to talk it through

A diary shows you the shape of things. Working out what to do about it is what a session is for — the first conversation costs you nothing.
