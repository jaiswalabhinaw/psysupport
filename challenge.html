Why a quiz

Why we do not score anyone on their mood

A bad week is not a low score

Ranking people on how they feel would put whoever is struggling most at the bottom every week. That is the opposite of what this is for.

Nobody has to perform

Because the quiz is about knowledge, there is no reason to pretend you are fine to do well. You can be having an awful week and still win.

Your diary stays private

The mood diary never leaves your phone and has nothing to do with the leaderboard. Only a quiz score is ever shared.
